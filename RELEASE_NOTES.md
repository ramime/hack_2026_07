## 🎯 Release Summary

Release `v0.2.0` introduces the **Team Lead Cockpit**, complete with a real-time budget heatmap, traffic-light budget drift alerts (>80% Warning / >100% Danger), and an interactive **AI Time & Budget Estimator Modal** for deliverable forecasting.

### Key Highlights
- **Team Lead Cockpit (`/teamlead`)**: Real-time budget heatmap showing target budget vs actual spend per campaign.
- **Traffic Light Alerts**: Automatic status classification and progress bars for Ritter Sport (Green), Bosch (Yellow), and Porsche (Red).
- **AI Time & Budget Estimator**: Interactive effort calculator with live deliverable forecasting.
- **Role Navigation**: Navigation header links between Zeiterfassung (`/`) and Team Lead Cockpit (`/teamlead`).

# RELEASE_NOTES.md — AgencyPulse v0.5.1

**Release Date:** 2026-07-25  
**Milestone:** Protected Client Portal & Pitch Slides (`v0.5.1`)

## 🎯 Release Summary

Release `v0.5.1` delivers the official **Protected Client Portal** (`/portal/c/<token>`) along with full pitch slide integration (`/slides`), enabling agency clients to securely view campaign progress while pitch presenters demo the feature.

### Key Highlights
- **Cryptic Client Links**: Anonymized URLs per client (e.g. `/portal/c/ritter-sport-8821`, `/portal/c/bosch-4492`, `/portal/c/porsche-9102`).
- **4-Digit PIN Security Gate**: HTMX PIN entry form with real-time authentication and session cookie management.
- **Client Transparency Dashboard**: Real-time campaign budget progress bars and delivered content assets feed (TikToks, Reels, 3D Renders, Masters).
- **Pitch Deck Integration**: Included Client Portal overview slide in `/slides` presentation viewer.
- **Business Confidentiality Shield**: Internal staff cost rates, billing margins, and individual employee identities are strictly hidden from client view.
- **One-Click Staff Access**: Direct "Open Portal" links added to Team Lead and Executive cockpits.
